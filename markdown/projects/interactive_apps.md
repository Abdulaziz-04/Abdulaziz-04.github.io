### Math Garden - Interactive Toddler App
Math Garden - Interactive Toddler App
A browser-based addition game for toddlers and early learners
where they write the answer by hand, a small MLP neural network
recognizes the digit in real time, and a colorful garden grows or
shrinks based on their answers.

### Tech Stack
Tech Stack:
Python, Tensorflow, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, TensorFlow.js
Learn more!

### Mood Synergizer - Emotion Aware Music Player
Mood Synergizer - Emotion Aware Music Player
Desktop application that reads a user’s facial expression from the
webcam, detects their dominant emotion, and instantly launches a
curated playlist that matches the mood. Designed as a playful way
for students to unwind after work or studying.

### Tech Stack
Tech Stack:
Python, Tkinter, OpenCV, DeepFace, Pillow, Pygame
Learn more!
